How to read this page
Price
The odds at the sportsbook. −110 means bet 110 to win 100. +150 means bet 100 to win 150.
Fair price
What the odds would be if the sportsbook took no cut. Like a coin flip paying even money.
Edge
How the price compares to the fair price. Top Pick is +2% or better. Green is 0% to +1.9%. Yellow is a close call, a little under 0%.
